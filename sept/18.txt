             Java object
                 │
                 ├── Intrinsic monitor 🔒
                 │       │
                 │       ├── synchronized
                 │       ├── wait()
                 │       ├── notify()
                 │       └── notifyAll()
                 │
                 └── Can ALSO be used as a normal object


                vs


                 ReentrantLock object
        │
        ├── Intrinsic monitor 🔒
        │      └── synchronized(lock)
        │
        └── ReentrantLock mechanism 🔒
               ├── lock()
               ├── unlock()
               └── Condition.await()/signal()





               the intrinsic monitor is the synchronization mechanism used by synchronized. 👍




you can not restart the treminated thread
